Straight answers about Ordora's digital menu, ordering, and pricing. Prices below are as shown on the pricing pages at the time of writing (October 2026) — check the live pricing pages before deciding.

01Do my guests need an app to order from Ordora?

No. Ordora opens in the guest's phone browser via a link or QR code. Nothing to download, no account to create.

02Does Ordora charge a commission on orders?

No. Ordora charges one fixed monthly subscription and takes no commission on restaurant orders.

03How much does Ordora cost?

Ordora has one monthly plan, shown on the Pricing page, and currently 4.99 US$ per month. The price shown on the pricing page is the current price — check it before subscribing.

04Where do orders arrive?

Orders appear in the restaurant dashboard, and their summaries can be shared on WhatsApp.

05Can I use my restaurant's colors and brand?

Yes. Every restaurant's page carries its name, logo, cover photos, colors, currency, and menu photos.

Every restaurant gets its own clean link like /restaurant?r=your-slug.

06What does an Ordora subscription include?

Ordora's single plan at 4.99 US$ per month includes the digital menu and online ordering (pickup or delivery) and the restaurant dashboard, with no commission on orders, and a 7-day free trial, no card required at sign-up. The price shown on the pricing page is the current price.

See About Ordora page for more.

07Do customers pay by card through Ordora?

Ordering with Ordora is a menu-and-order flow: the guest sends their order to the restaurant, and payment is arranged with the restaurant as it is today. Ordora does not add card payments in this plan.

08How do I start?

Try Demo menu, then Start for free or Contact us And we'll help you set up your page.
