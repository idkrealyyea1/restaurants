Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about Ordora's digital menu, ordering, pricing, and how it compares with MenuX. Prices below are the ones shown on the pricing pages at the time of writing (October 2026) — check the live pricing pages before deciding.

Do my guests need an app?

No. Ordora opens in the guest's mobile browser from a link or QR code. There is nothing to download and no account to create.

Does Ordora charge a commission on orders?

No. One flat monthly subscription, no commission on your restaurant's orders, and no hidden fees.

How much does Ordora cost?

One monthly plan, currently $4.99 per month as shown on the pricing page. If the pricing page ever shows a different figure, that figure is the current one.

Where do orders arrive?

Orders appear in your restaurant dashboard, and you can share their summaries through WhatsApp.

Can I use my restaurant's colors and identity?

Yes. Your page carries your name, logo, cover images, colors, currency, and menu photos, and each restaurant gets its own clean link such as /restaurant/your-slug.

How is Ordora different from MenuX?

Ordora includes online ordering in its single $4.99 per month plan. On MenuX, the free tier is a display menu only and the basic paid tier does not include ordering; ordering starts with the Excellence tier, listed at $14.9 per month when last checked (October 2026). MenuX prices can change — compare the current pricing pages before choosing. See the full Ordora vs MenuX comparison.

Do customers pay by card through Ordora?

Ordering through Ordora is a menu-and-order flow: the guest sends the order to the restaurant, and payment is arranged with the restaurant as today. Ordora does not add a card-payment gateway in this plan.

How do I start?

Try the demo menu, then start free or contact us and we will help set up your page.
