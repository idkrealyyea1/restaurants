How Ordora works

Three steps, working on any phone, with no customer account needed.

01

Create your restaurant

We create for you /restaurant?r=your-slug. Add the logo, cover, categories, items, and prices. It takes about 10 minutes. Images are saved in Postgres, not on a temporary disk.

Tip: start with 8–12 items, and add more after your first order.

02

Share your menu

Share the link in your Instagram bio, WhatsApp status, and Google page. Print QR codes for tables and packaging. Use the share page and QR in the dashboard.

One link that works everywhere — no separate app.

03

Receive orders

The customer builds the cart and sends it. You get instant alerts in the dashboard + an organized order with a tracking code. Update the status so the customer can track it via /track.

Delivery fees and opening hours are enforced server-side.

QR code←Menu←Item←Cart←Orders + WhatsApp

What the customer sees

  1. Scans the QR or opens the link → cover, logo, open badge, and categories
  2. Browses the menu, taps + On the price card — a fly-to-cart animation
  3. Opens the cart sheet: quantity buttons, pickup/delivery toggle, address if delivery
  4. Enters name and WhatsApp (+ address for delivery), sends the order or opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled cart
  5. Gets a tracking code — tracks the status via /track?code=XXXX
