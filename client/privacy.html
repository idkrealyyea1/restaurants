Your privacy

Privacy Policy, in short.

What we collect, why we collect it, and who sees it — without jargon.

Start for free What the menu looks like →

Last updated: November 2026

01What we collect

From restaurant owners: the username, business details, menu content, and settings you enter. From guests when ordering: the name, phone or WhatsApp number, delivery address, and order details. And technically: your language preference and cart contents, stored on your device, plus usage information collected by Microsoft Clarity, which can include interaction recordings, device information and cookies.

02How we use it

Only to run the service: show the menu, carry the order to the restaurant, display it in the restaurant's dashboard, and help restaurants manage their pages. We do not use guest information for advertising.

03Who sees it

A guest's order details go to the restaurant they ordered from, and to the hosting and database providers that operate Ordora. We do not sell personal information to anyone.

04WhatsApp

When a guest chooses to send a copy of their order via WhatsApp, the message opens on their own phone and they send it themselves to the restaurant's number. WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy apply to that conversation.

05How long we keep it

Restaurant data stays while the account is active, and order records stay so the restaurant can serve its guests and track its business. If a restaurant closes its account, contact us and we will delete what the law does not require us to keep.

06Your choices

Guests order without an account. To correct or delete information about you, message us on WhatsApp from the contact page. Restaurant owners can edit their page content from their dashboard at any time.

07Security

We protect the service with encryption in transit, hashed passwords, and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your password private.

08Changes and contact

We may update this policy, and the current version will always be on this page with its date. Have a question? Message us on WhatsApp from the contact page.
