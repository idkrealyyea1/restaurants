Cafes move fast — and the menu has to keep up

  • Counter QR: Guests scan while they wait in line and pre-order for pickup.
  • Types as items: List latte / iced latte / oat latte as separate items with photos.
  • No account: Regular customers reorder with their tracking code — no sign-in needed.
  • Currency and fees: Set Jordanian dinars / Saudi riyals, and a delivery fee if you deliver.
