About Ordora

Your restaurant's digital menu, And online ordering, simply.

Ordora is a digital menu in your restaurant's brand: your guests open it from a link or QR code, browse the items, and order for pickup or delivery — no app to download, no account to create.

What is Ordora?

Every restaurant gets its own public menu page, with a clean link like /restaurant?r=your-slug, shares it via a link or prints its QR code on the tables. The guest opens the page in their phone browser, sees the items with photos and prices, adds what they want to the cart, and sends the order. Orders appear in the restaurant dashboard, their summaries can be shared via WhatsApp, and the guest can track their order with the tracking code and reorder the same items from the confirmation screen or the tracking page.

Ordora for restaurants, cafes, dessert shops, and cake shops — one restaurant, one page, one monthly price.

Designed English-first, with your restaurant's brand first

  • ✓ English-first, left-to-right: The interface is English by default, with Arabic one tap away, and the menu is designed for easy reading on phones.
  • ✓ Your restaurant's brand, not the platform's: Your restaurant's name, logo, cover photos, colors, currency, and menu photos on its page.
  • ✓ Designed mobile-first: A fast-loading vertical menu, fixed categories at the top of the page, and a clear cart — because most guests open it from their phones.
  • ✓ QR code for each restaurant: One link you print and stick on tables and the storefront — it opens the menu directly.
  • ✓ “Open now” badge and opening hours: The badge is calculated from your restaurant's opening hours, with today's hours visible to guests.
  • ✓ Item details: Photos, prices, badges (best seller, new, spicy, chef's pick, vegetarian), calories, prep time, allergy notes, and a struck-through old price with the saving where needed.

Its benefits

  • ✓ One fixed monthly price: 4.99 US$ per month. No tiers, no surprises in the price.
  • ✓ 7-day free trial, no card required at sign-up. Create your account and try it before you pay anything.
  • ✓ No commission on orders. What your guests pay stays with your restaurant; Ordora only charges the subscription.
  • ✓ Full online ordering: Cart, pickup or delivery with a delivery fee set by the restaurant, and a notes field for the order.
  • ✓ Order summaries via WhatsApp: Share the order summary with the restaurant or the guest on WhatsApp.
  • ✓ Live dashboard: Menu, orders, table bookings, and analytics for orders, revenue, and best-selling items.
  • ✓ Tracking codes for orders: Guests know their order status with their code, and can easily reorder the same order.
  • ✓ Optional promo banner: Show a promo banner above your menu whenever you want, and hide it whenever you want.

Honestly: the current plan's limits

This plan does not include a card payment gateway, a standalone bookings app, a standalone promotions app, multi-branch management, or multiple admin roles. Payment is arranged with the restaurant as it is today, and bookings are managed from the same dashboard.

How to start?

  1. Create your restaurant account and choose its link (slug).
  2. Add categories and items with prices and photos, and set your opening hours.
  3. Set your brand: logo, cover photos, colors, currency, delivery fee.
  4. Share the link or print a QR code, and receive orders in the dashboard.

Try it yourself before you decide

The demo menu is open to everyone, and the price is shown transparently.
