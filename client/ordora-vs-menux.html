Ordora vs MenuX — an honest comparison

Checked by visiting MenuX's public pricing page as a visitor on 24 October 2026, without creating an account. Prices change — re-check both pricing pages on the day you decide. This page makes no claim that Ordora is the best for every restaurant; it states the price difference that matters most for ordering.

The headline difference

Ordora includes online ordering in its single plan at $4.99 per month. On MenuX, ordering is not in the free tier (a display menu only, 15 items) and not in the Basic tier ($9.9 per month); ordering starts with the Excellence tier at $14.9 per month. That is roughly three times Ordora's monthly price for the ability to take orders at all.

What Ordora includes at $4.99 per month

  • Digital menu with a clean link per restaurant and QR code
  • Online ordering: cart, pickup or delivery, WhatsApp order summaries
  • Restaurant dashboard: menu, orders, table bookings, analytics
  • Your branding: logo, cover images, colors, currency
  • Item details: badges, calories, prep time, allergen notes, old price with "save" label
  • Opening hours with an open/closed badge, and an optional promo banner
  • No commission on orders

What MenuX offers at higher tiers

MenuX is a polished, larger platform with features Ordora does not have in this plan: a booking app and a promotions app (Comprehensive tier, $19.9 per month), up to 10 admin accounts, and multi-language menu display. If you need those, MenuX's higher tiers may fit you better — this page does not hide that.

What Ordora does not do

Ordora in this plan does not include a card-payment gateway, a booking app, a promotions app, multi-branch management, or multiple admin roles. It is one restaurant, one page, one monthly price.

Bottom line

If you mainly need a menu guests can open and order from, Ordora gives you that for $4.99 per month, where MenuX's ordering starts at $14.9 per month. Try the demo menu and compare the live Ordora pricing with MenuX's pricing page before you choose.
