How to create a digital menu for your restaurant

A digital menu is a public menu page for your restaurant that customers open from a link or QR code in their phone's browser — no app, no account. Here's how to create one with Ordora, step by step:

  1. Create your restaurant account and choose the link (slug): This link is the address of your public menu, like /restaurant?r=your-restaurant-name.
  2. Add categories and items: Split the menu (e.g. grills, appetizers, desserts), and add each item's name, price, and photo, and set its availability.
  3. Set your brand and opening hours: Logo, cover photos, colors, and currency, plus opening hours that show the open/closed badge automatically.
  4. Share the link and print a QR code: Put the link in your Instagram bio and WhatsApp status, and print a QR code for tables and packaging — updating the menu needs no reprinting.
  5. Receive orders: Orders appear in the restaurant dashboard (pickup or delivery), their summary can be shared on WhatsApp, and customers track their order with its code.

How much does it cost? 7-day free trial, no card required at sign-up, then a single monthly subscription of $4.99, with no commission on orders. Does the customer need an app? No — the browser only. Try it yourself from Demo menu.
