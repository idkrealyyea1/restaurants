How to organize WhatsApp orders for your restaurant or home business

Do your orders arrive scattered across WhatsApp messages, Instagram comments, and phone calls? This guide shows you how to gather them into one menu, receive every order organized, and send its summary on WhatsApp in one tap — without losing a single order among the messages.

Why do WhatsApp orders get lost?

The problem isn't WhatsApp itself — the problem is that the order has no single fixed place to be written down.

Scattered messages

Orders arrive as a comment, a message, a phone call — each in a different format. And it's very easy for an order to get lost among them, or to be misunderstood.

The same questions every day

How much? Is there delivery? What's available today? You answer them one by one, instead of having them written in one place everyone can see.

No record, no tracking

When the order isn't written in one fixed place, neither you nor the customer knows where it stands — and you end up asking “Which order?” and “Where is it now?”.

The fix in five steps

The idea is simple: customers order from the menu, not from the chat — and WhatsApp stays the confirmation channel, not the order book.

01

Gather your menu into one link

Create your restaurant menu and take its public link, like /restaurant?r=your-business-name. Put it in your Instagram bio and WhatsApp status, and print the QR code on packaging and tables. Any update to prices or items appears instantly — no reprinting needed.

02

Let customers order from the menu itself

The customer opens the link in their phone's browser, browses categories, prices, and availability, and builds their cart — no app, no account.

03

Receive an organized order

The order reaches your dashboard complete: items and quantities, pickup or delivery, name and WhatsApp number — with a tracking code.

04

Send the order summary on WhatsApp

After the order succeeds, if the owner's WhatsApp number is set, the customer moves to WhatsApp with a ready message containing the order, and taps Send. That way the summary arrives on WhatsApp organized, instead of as an incomplete free-form message.

05

Update the status and let them track it

Update the order status from the dashboard, and customers track it with their tracking code via the tracking page — without asking you “Where's my order?” every few minutes.

What actually changes in practice?

  • One menu that answers price and availability questions before they're asked.
  • Every order is written in a single record, not spread across ten chats.
  • WhatsApp stays the confirmation and communication channel — not the order book.
  • And when your business grows, the same link grows with you — you don't need to start from scratch.
